🤰 Prenatal Care Guide

Important tips for a healthy pregnancy

✅ Do's

  • Take folic acid daily (as prescribed)
  • Attend all ANC checkups
  • Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily
  • Eat iron & calcium rich food
  • Get 8 hours of sleep
  • Light walking daily

❌ Don'ts

  • Avoid alcohol & smoking
  • Don't skip meals
  • Avoid raw/undercooked meat
  • Don't lift heavy objects
  • Avoid self-medication
  • Don't ignore bleeding/pain

🚨 Emergency Signs — Go to hospital immediately

  • Severe headache or blurred vision
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • No baby movement for hours
